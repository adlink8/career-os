## 📝 2024-12 写作真题：幸福与应对问题的能力

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the saying "Happiness is not the absence of problems, but the ability to deal with them." You can give examples to illustrate your point. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
Happiness is a universal pursuit, yet many people mistakenly believe that being happy means having no problems at all. However, as the saying goes, "Happiness is not the absence of problems, but the ability to deal with them."

In reality, problems are an inevitable part of life. What truly matters is how we face and handle them. For instance, students who struggle with difficult subjects but work hard to improve their understanding often feel a deeper sense of satisfaction than those who breeze through easy courses. Similarly, entrepreneurs who overcome business challenges develop resilience and find greater fulfillment in their achievements.

The ability to deal with problems builds confidence and character. When we successfully navigate through difficulties, we gain valuable experience and become better equipped for future challenges. This growth mindset is the true source of lasting happiness.

In conclusion, rather than wishing for a problem-free life, we should focus on developing our problem-solving skills. It is through overcoming obstacles that we find genuine happiness and meaning in life.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- universal pursuit | inevitable part of life | sense of satisfaction | builds confidence and character | growth mindset | overcoming obstacles

💡 **【备考技巧】**：注意使用举例论证，可从学习、工作、生活等角度展开。结尾应呼应开头观点。

## 📝 2024-06 写作真题：责任感的重要性

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the importance of having a sense of responsibility. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
In today's fast-paced world, having a sense of responsibility is more important than ever. Responsibility is not just a moral quality; it is the foundation of personal growth and social harmony.

First, responsibility is essential for personal development. When individuals take responsibility for their actions, they learn from their mistakes and become more reliable. For example, students who are responsible for their studies tend to achieve better results and develop stronger self-discipline.

Second, responsibility strengthens relationships. In families, responsible members contribute to a stable and loving environment. In workplaces, responsible employees earn trust and respect from their colleagues and supervisors, which often leads to better career opportunities.

Finally, responsibility is crucial for society as a whole. When citizens fulfill their civic duties, such as obeying laws and protecting the environment, the entire community benefits. A responsible society is a prosperous and peaceful one.

In short, cultivating a sense of responsibility benefits individuals, relationships, and society. It is a quality that everyone should strive to develop.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- fast-paced world | foundation of personal growth | social harmony | learn from mistakes | self-discipline | civic duties

💡 **【备考技巧】**：采用"总-分-总"结构。分论点可从个人、家庭/职场、社会三个层面展开。

## 📝 2023-12 写作真题：克服怀疑，拥抱未来

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the saying "The only limit to our realization of tomorrow will be our doubts of today." You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
Franklin D. Roosevelt once said, "The only limit to our realization of tomorrow will be our doubts of today." This profound statement reminds us that self-doubt is often the biggest obstacle standing between us and our dreams.

Doubt can paralyze us and prevent us from taking action. When we constantly question our abilities, we miss valuable opportunities for growth. For instance, many talented individuals never pursue their passions because they doubt whether they can succeed. This hesitation becomes a self-fulfilling prophecy.

However, history is filled with examples of people who overcame their doubts and achieved greatness. Thomas Edison failed thousands of times before inventing the light bulb, but he never doubted his ultimate success. His persistence and confidence in his vision transformed the world.

To realize our potential, we must learn to manage our doubts. Setting realistic goals, seeking support from others, and celebrating small victories can help build confidence. By replacing doubt with determination, we open doors to endless possibilities.

Let us not allow today's doubts to limit tomorrow's achievements.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- profound statement | self-fulfilling prophecy | overcame their doubts | persistence and confidence | realize our potential | endless possibilities

💡 **【备考技巧】**：引用名言开头，分析怀疑的危害，用正面例子论证克服怀疑的重要性。

## 📝 2023-06 写作真题：培养良好习惯的重要性

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the importance of developing good habits. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
Good habits are the building blocks of a successful and fulfilling life. As the saying goes, "We are what we repeatedly do." Developing positive habits can have a profound impact on our health, productivity, and overall well-being.

First, good health habits are fundamental. Regular exercise, a balanced diet, and adequate sleep contribute to physical fitness and mental clarity. People who maintain these habits are more energetic and less prone to illness, enabling them to perform better in all areas of life.

Second, good study and work habits boost productivity. Time management, setting priorities, and maintaining focus are habits that help us accomplish more in less time. Students who develop consistent study routines tend to achieve higher grades, while professionals with good work habits advance faster in their careers.

Third, good interpersonal habits enrich our relationships. Being a good listener, showing empathy, and communicating effectively are habits that strengthen bonds with family, friends, and colleagues.

In conclusion, developing good habits requires effort and consistency, but the rewards are immeasurable. Start building positive habits today, and they will shape a brighter tomorrow.
`

- building blocks | profound impact | physical fitness and mental clarity | time management | interpersonal habits | effort and consistency

💡 **【备考技巧】**：从健康、学习/工作、人际关系三个角度论述。用具体例子支撑论点。

## 📝 2022-12 写作真题：阅读能力的重要性及培养方法

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the importance of reading ability and how to develop it. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
Reading is one of the most valuable skills a person can possess. In the information age, strong reading ability is essential for academic success, career development, and personal growth.

The importance of reading cannot be overstated. First, it expands our knowledge and broadens our horizons. Through reading, we can explore different cultures, ideas, and perspectives without leaving our homes. Second, reading improves our critical thinking and analytical skills. When we engage with complex texts, we learn to evaluate information and form our own opinions. Third, reading enhances our vocabulary and communication skills, which are crucial in both academic and professional settings.

To develop reading ability, consistency is key. Setting aside dedicated reading time each day, even just 30 minutes, can make a significant difference over time. Starting with materials that match our interests and reading level helps build confidence. Additionally, taking notes, discussing books with others, and gradually challenging ourselves with more complex texts can accelerate improvement.

In summary, reading is a lifelong skill that opens doors to knowledge and opportunities. By cultivating good reading habits, we invest in our future success.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- information age | broadens our horizons | critical thinking and analytical skills | consistency is key | lifelong skill | invest in our future

💡 **【备考技巧】**：先论述重要性（知识、思维、表达），再给出培养方法（坚持、兴趣、渐进）。

## 📝 2021-12 写作真题：科技会取代教师吗？

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on whether technology will replace teachers. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
With the rapid development of technology, some people wonder whether teachers will eventually be replaced by computers and artificial intelligence. While technology has transformed education in many ways, I believe that teachers will always be indispensable.

Technology certainly offers many advantages in education. Online courses, educational apps, and AI tutors provide students with access to vast amounts of information and personalized learning experiences. These tools can supplement classroom teaching and make learning more efficient.

However, teachers offer something that technology cannot replicate: human connection. A good teacher inspires students, understands their individual needs, and provides emotional support. Teachers serve as role models and mentors, helping students develop not just academic skills but also values, character, and social skills.

Moreover, teaching involves complex tasks that require human judgment, such as adapting lesson plans to suit different learning styles, managing classroom dynamics, and motivating struggling students. These are areas where human teachers excel and technology falls short.

In conclusion, technology is a powerful tool that can enhance education, but it cannot replace the irreplaceable role of teachers. The future of education lies in the combination of technology and human expertise.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- rapid development of technology | indispensable | personalized learning | human connection | role models and mentors | human judgment

💡 **【备考技巧】**：辩证分析，先承认科技优势，再强调教师不可替代的作用，最后总结两者结合。

## 📝 2021-06 写作真题：良好师生关系的重要性

## 📝 2020-12 写作真题：交通方式的变化

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on changes in the way of transportation. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
Over the past few decades, the way people travel has undergone significant changes. From traditional bicycles and buses to modern high-speed trains and electric vehicles, transportation has become faster, greener, and more convenient.

In the past, most people relied on bicycles or public buses for daily commuting. Long-distance travel was time-consuming and often uncomfortable. Trains were slow, and air travel was a luxury that few could afford. The limited transportation options restricted people's mobility and opportunities.

Today, the situation has changed dramatically. High-speed railways connect major cities in just a few hours, making business trips and family visits much easier. Ride-sharing services and electric scooters provide flexible options for short-distance travel. Moreover, the rise of electric vehicles reflects a growing awareness of environmental protection.

These changes have brought numerous benefits. People enjoy greater freedom of movement, businesses can operate more efficiently, and the environment benefits from cleaner transportation options. However, challenges remain, such as traffic congestion in cities and the need for better infrastructure in rural areas.

Looking ahead, innovations like autonomous vehicles and hyperloop technology promise to further revolutionize transportation. The journey toward smarter, more sustainable mobility continues.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- undergone significant changes | time-consuming | environmental protection | freedom of movement | traffic congestion | sustainable mobility

💡 **【备考技巧】**：采用对比结构：过去→现在→未来。用具体例子说明变化。

## 📝 2020-09 写作真题：学习基本生活技能的重要性

**【题目要求】**：
> For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the importance of learning basic life skills. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

**【高分范文】**：
`	ext
In an era of rapid technological advancement, some young people neglect basic life skills such as cooking, cleaning, and financial management. However, these skills are essential for independent living and personal well-being.

First, basic life skills promote self-reliance. When young people leave home for college or work, they need to take care of themselves. Those who can cook nutritious meals, manage their finances, and maintain a clean living space are better prepared for adult life. Without these skills, they may struggle with daily challenges and feel overwhelmed.

Second, learning life skills builds problem-solving abilities. When we cook a meal, fix a broken appliance, or organize our schedule, we are practicing critical thinking and creativity. These skills transfer to other areas of life, including academic and professional pursuits.

Third, basic life skills contribute to mental health. People who can manage their daily lives effectively experience less stress and greater satisfaction. The sense of accomplishment from completing everyday tasks boosts self-esteem and confidence.

Schools and families should work together to teach young people these essential skills. By combining academic education with practical life skills training, we can help the next generation lead healthier, more fulfilling lives.
`

**【核心提分亮点词组】**：
- technological advancement | self-reliance | problem-solving abilities | mental health | sense of accomplishment | practical life skills

💡 **【备考技巧】**：从自立能力、问题解决、心理健康三个角度论证。提出学校和家庭的责任。
