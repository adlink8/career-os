## 📖 Reading Passage: The Power of Curiosity: How Questioning Drives Scientific Breakthroughs (好奇心的神经机制：为什么提问能够驱动突破性科学创新)

Throughout the history of human civilization, humanity's most transformative intellectual revolutions—from the formulation of universal gravitation to the discovery of penicillin—were rarely sparked by the mechanical execution of assigned tasks. Rather, they were catalyzed by an insatiable, spontaneous cognitive hunger: curiosity. Long regarded as an enigmatic personality quirk, curiosity is now being rigorously decoded by cognitive psychologists and neuroscientists. Far from being a trivial diversion, curiosity is the primary biological engine driving intellectual exploration, neurochemical learning acceleration, and paradigm-shifting scientific innovation.

In a foundational theoretical model, behavioral economist George Loewenstein conceptualized curiosity as an 'information gap.' Loewenstein posited that curiosity is triggered whenever an individual perceives a frustrating discrepancy between what they currently know and what they desire to know. This cognitive discrepancy functions neurochemically much like physical hunger or thirst: it induces an unpleasant state of mental deprivation that relentlessly compels an individual to seek epistemic satisfaction. Solving the mystery eliminates the tension, delivering an exhilarating sensation of cognitive closure.

Modern functional brain imaging has illuminated the profound neurobiological architecture underpinning this phenomenon. When individuals encounter an intriguing mystery, functional MRI scans reveal simultaneous activation in two critical neural hubs: the striatum—the brain's primary dopamine-driven reward and motivation circuit—and the hippocampus, the neurological epicenter responsible for memory consolidation. In essence, curiosity primes the brain for optimal learning. When an individual is genuinely curious about an anomaly, the brain floods synapses with dopamine, radically enhancing attention, accelerating information processing, and cementing subsequent factual recall far more effectively than external academic coercion.

Tragically, institutional education systems and standardized corporate workplaces frequently suppress the very curiosity they claim to value. Rigid academic curricula prioritize memorizing predetermined answers for standardized tests, penalizing students who ask unconventional, open-ended questions. Cultivating groundbreaking innovation requires designing environments that celebrate productive failure and reward bold intellectual inquiry. We must recognize that the hallmark of brilliant minds is not the sheer volume of answers they regurgitate, but the audacity and depth of the questions they dare to ask.

### 🏮 全文逐段地道精翻

贯穿人类文明演进史，那些最具颠覆性的人类智力革命——从万有引力定律的奠基到青霉素的偶然发现——极少是按部就班机械完成既定指令的产物。相反，它们无一例外都是由一种难以遏制的自发认知渴求所点燃的：那就是好奇心。长期以来，好奇心被视为一种难以捉摸的古怪性格特质；而如今，认知心理学家与神经科学家正在用硬核科学严谨破译它的奥秘。好奇心绝非可有可无的闲情逸致，它是驱动智力探险、神经化学学习加速以及颠覆性科学创新的终极生物学引擎。

在一项奠基性的理论模型中，行为经济学家乔治·洛温斯坦（George Loewenstein）将好奇心精辟地定义为一种“信息差（information gap）”。洛温斯坦指出，每当一个人敏锐察觉到“自己已知”与“渴望所知”之间存在着令人抓狂的认知缺口时，好奇心就会被瞬间引爆。这种认知落差在神经生物学层面的生理机制，与肉体上的饥肠辘辘或干渴难耐惊人相似：它在大脑中制造出一种令人煎熬的心智匮乏感，如同无形的皮鞭无情驱使个体去狂热探求认知的满足。唯有解开谜团化解悬念，这种神经紧绷才能得以释放，带来认知闭环时无与伦比的欣悦狂喜。

现代功能性脑成像技术彻底揭示了这一奇妙现象背后的深层神经生物学底座。当受试者邂逅一个引人入胜的未解谜题时，功能性磁共振成像清晰显示出大脑两大核心枢纽的同步剧烈激活：负责多巴胺奖赏与内在动机中枢的“纹状体”，以及负责记忆巩固的神经指挥中枢“海马体”。从本质上讲，好奇心是大脑实现顶级学习状态的最佳预热器。当一个人对某一反常现象产生真挚好奇时，大脑会向神经突触大剂量泵送多巴胺，从而颠覆性地拔高专注力、成倍加速信息解析，并以远超任何外部学业高压逼迫的效率，将随后获得的事实印记深深刻入长期记忆中。

然而极其讽刺的是，现代体制化的学校教育与死板的企业职场，却往往在系统性扼杀它们口口声声宣称崇尚的好奇心。僵化的应试课纲将机械背诵标准化考试的既定标准答案奉为唯一圭臬，甚至无情惩罚那些提出离经叛道、开放式探索问题的求知者。培育具有颠覆性的时代创新，必须敢于搭建包容尝试性失败、重奖大胆思想探求的宽容土壤。我们必须深刻清醒地认识到：卓越伟大心智的真正勋章，从来不是看他们能机械反刍倒背多少标准答案，而是在于他们到底敢于提出何等深邃、何等大胆的时代之问！
