## 📖 Reading Passage: Algorithmic Bias: When Machine Learning Perpetuates Human Inequity (算法偏见：机器学习如何加剧人类历史不平等)

As artificial intelligence systems are rapidly integrated into public administration, judicial sentencing, financial lending, and corporate recruitment, automated algorithms are frequently promoted as impartial arbiters. Enthusiasts argue that replacing flawed human discretion with cold mathematical formulas eliminates personal prejudice, nepotism, and emotional bias. By analyzing colossal datasets at computational speeds, machine-learning models promise to render institutional decision-making wholly meritocratic. However, computer scientists and sociologists have uncovered a troubling reality: algorithms do not automatically eliminate discrimination; rather, they frequently codify, scale, and disguise existing societal inequities under a veneer of mathematical objectivity.

The fundamental source of algorithmic bias lies within the training data used to educate neural networks. Machine learning algorithms identify statistical correlations from historical records. When historical datasets reflect decades of systemic socioeconomic disadvantages, racial discrimination, or gender disparities, the predictive algorithm faithfully reproduces and institutionalizes those historical patterns. For example, when a prominent multinational technology enterprise deployed an automated resume-screening tool trained on a decade of past hiring data, the system systematically downgraded applications containing the word 'women's,' reflecting the male-dominated historical composition of the tech workforce.

Complicating matters further is the notorious 'black box' dilemma. Modern deep-learning architectures comprise millions of interconnected mathematical parameters, making it virtually impossible even for their creators to retrace the precise causal logic behind a specific automated verdict. When an applicant is denied a mortgage, or a job seeker is silently filtered out by an algorithm, affected individuals are rarely provided with actionable explanations or meaningful avenues for appeal. What was once visible human prejudice has been laundered into an inscrutable computational verdict, insulating powerful institutions from legal accountability.

Combating algorithmic bias requires confronting the dangerous myth of mathematical neutrality. Technologists must implement rigorous pre-deployment audits, curate representative training datasets, and actively balance algorithms to offset historical skews. Furthermore, governments must establish enforceable algorithmic accountability legislation, mandating explainability and human oversight in all automated decisions that affect basic human rights. Artificial intelligence should serve as an instrument for social elevation, not an automated accelerator of historic injustice.

### 逐题深度深度解析与陷阱剖析

**第 1 题 (正确答案: B)**
- **题干定位**：1. What is the main argument made by advocates of automated algorithms in Paragraph 1?
- **命题解密**：第一段第 2 句明确指出算法狂热推崇者的观点：`Enthusiasts argue that replacing flawed human discretion with cold mathematical formulas eliminates personal prejudice, nepotism, and emotional bias. By analyzing colossal datasets at computational speeds, machine-learning models promise to render institutional decision-making wholly meritocratic.`
- **选项对齐**：原文 `replacing flawed human discretion with cold mathematical formulas eliminates personal prejudice... wholly meritocratic` 与 B 项 `Replacing human judgment with formulas removes prejudice and ensures fairness` 构成完美同义转换（`discretion` 对应 `judgment`，`eliminates prejudice` 对应 `removes prejudice`，`wholly meritocratic` 对应 `ensures fairness`）。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项 `cheaper to build than hiring personnel` 属于主观臆造的财务成本干扰；
  - C 项 `eliminate the need for legal systems` 夸大其词，原文仅说引入司法量刑；
  - D 项 `expressing genuine human empathy` 与原文“冷冰冰的数学公式（cold mathematical formulas）”截然相反。

**第 2 题 (正确答案: A)**
- **题干定位**：2. The word "veneer" in Paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to _______.
- **命题解密**：第一段末句揭示客观现实：`algorithms do not automatically eliminate discrimination; rather, they frequently codify, scale, and disguise existing societal inequities under a veneer of mathematical objectivity.`
- **选项对齐**：句中谓语动词为 `disguise`（伪装、掩盖），宾语为社会不公（`existing societal inequities`），在所谓“数学客观性”的外表掩护下掩盖不平等。`veneer` 意为“虚假的外表、虚饰的包装”，精准对应 A 项 `superficial disguise`（表面伪装）。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - B 项 `solid foundation`（坚实基础，反义干扰）；
  - C 项 `complete absence`（完全缺失）；
  - D 项 `revolutionary theory`（革命性理论）均与批判算法伪善的语境不合。

**第 3 题 (正确答案: C)**
- **题干定位**：3. Why did the tech company's resume-screening tool downgrade applications with the word "women's"?
- **命题解密**：第二段末句给出具象案例：`when a prominent multinational technology enterprise deployed an automated resume-screening tool trained on a decade of past hiring data, the system systematically downgraded applications containing the word 'women's,' reflecting the male-dominated historical composition of the tech workforce.`
- **选项对齐**：系统歧视女性简历的根本原因在于模型是基于“过去十年的招聘数据”训练的，忠实复制了历史上男性主导的用工结构。这与 C 选项 `The algorithm was trained on historical data reflecting a male-dominated workforce` 完全一致。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项（女性候选人缺乏必要技术认证）纯属对受害者的无端归咎；
  - B 项（电脑病毒破坏数据库）属于低级无厘头干扰；
  - D 项（人类经理手动篡改评分）与原文的“自动化初筛工具（automated resume-screening tool）”自相矛盾。

**第 4 题 (正确答案: B)**
- **题干定位**：4. What makes the "black box" dilemma especially dangerous according to Paragraph 3?
- **命题解密**：第三段指出：深度学习模型参数极其复杂，连创造者都无法追溯因果逻辑（`making it virtually impossible even for their creators to retrace the precise causal logic`），直接后果是：`When an applicant is denied a mortgage, or a job seeker is silently filtered out by an algorithm, affected individuals are rarely provided with actionable explanations or meaningful avenues for appeal.`
- **选项对齐**：受影响的个体无法得知被拒的真实原因，也得不到申诉渠道，对应 B 项 `It prevents affected individuals from understanding decisions or seeking appeals`。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项（处理大文件时死机崩溃）纯属硬件故障无稽之谈；
  - C 项（让外国黑客窃取国家机密）、D 项（迫使企业在硬件上过度消费）均脱离了黑箱不可解释性导致社会救济缺失的讨论核心。

**第 5 题 (正确答案: C)**
- **题干定位**：5. What is essential to tackling algorithmic bias according to the author?
- **命题解密**：尾段集中阐述应对算法偏见的综合举措：
  1. `confronting the dangerous myth of mathematical neutrality`（打破数学中立迷思）；
  2. `implement rigorous pre-deployment audits, curate representative training datasets...`（执行严格的上线前审计）；
  3. `mandating explainability and human oversight in all automated decisions...`（强制推行可解释性与人类监管）。
- **选项对齐**：选项 C `Disrupting the myth of neutrality, conducting rigorous audits, and enforcing human oversight` 完美凝练了上述三层核心举措。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项 `Banning all forms of artificial intelligence immediately` 走向因噎废食的极端盲动；
  - B 项 `Allowing technology giants to handle audits without outside interference` 与原文呼吁政府立法监管（accountability legislation）针锋相对；
  - D 项（教导所有公民学习机器学习编程）属于无关泛化。
