## Part I / Writing / (30 minutes)

**Directions:** _Suppose your university is preparing a plan to make the whole campus all-digital by ending all printing and dropping printed textbooks. You are to write a letter to the president of your university to state your position, explain two major concerns about the plan, and offer one workable adjustment for each. You will have 30 minutes for the task. You should write at least <u>120</u> words but no more than <u>180</u> words._

### 评分要点（仿官方四档评分标准，原始分 15 分档，折算为试卷 106.5 分）

本题为"建议信"（letter of suggestion）类应用文，评分时重点核查四项任务完成度：**书信格式与语域得体、立场明确、两项顾虑各自对应一条改进建议、词数 120–180**。

- **第四档（13–15 分，折算 92.3–106.5 分）**：切题，称呼与落款完整，语气礼貌而立场清楚；两项顾虑切中无纸化方案的实际要害（如设备与网络条件差距、深度阅读与批注、断电或系统故障、长期存档与版权、残障与普通学生的打印依赖），并逐条给出一一对应的可操作调整；内容充实，段落分明；句式多变（让步、条件、虚拟、委婉疑问等），语法与选词只有 minor slips；上下文衔接自然。
- **第三档（7–12 分，折算 49.7–85.2 分）**：基本切题，格式大体正确，但顾虑与建议未一一对应，或建议笼统（如"学校应该多听取学生意见"）；句式较为单一，有若干语法错误但不影响理解；结构基本完整，连接手段使用欠熟练。
- **第二档（1–6 分，折算 7.1–42.6 分）**：未完成任务——只表态不列顾虑，或只抱怨不提调整，或丢弃书信格式与收信人写成普通议论文；词汇量有限，语法错误较多，部分句子无法理解；结构不完整或词数严重不足。
- **第一档（0 分）**：白卷；或通篇离题（如写成方案说明文、利弊罗列短文或致全体同学的倡议书）；所写内容太少、无法评判；抄袭试题或听力原文。

补充裁量：词数低于 120 或超过 180 酌情降半档至一档；缺称呼或落款降半档；顾虑与建议数量不足（少于两项顾虑，或未做到一项顾虑配一条建议）在第三档内就低评分；出现整句汉式英语堆砌（直译痕迹严重）在第三档内就低评分。

### 满分范文（约 160 词）

Dear President,

I am a sophomore in the School of Foreign Languages. Learning that the university plans to end all printing and drop printed textbooks, I welcome the direction but have two reservations, each with a suggested adjustment.

My first concern is fairness. **However carefully the plan is designed, students without a reliable device or a stable connection would fall behind from the first week of classes.** I therefore **recommend that the university keep printed copies of every core textbook available on loan and lend tablets to students in need.**

My second concern is deep reading. **Unless students can still write in the margins and flip back and forth freely, their grasp of long, demanding texts is bound to weaken.** **A workable adjustment would be a hybrid rule: printed readers for core courses and digital files for electives.**

**Would it be possible to pilot the plan in two schools before it is applied campus-wide?** I would be glad to join such a trial.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

（亮点句型说明：However + 副词/分词引导的让步状语从句；recommend that + (should) 动词原形的虚拟语气；Unless 条件句 + be bound to；would 表委婉建议的假想语气；Would it be possible to… 疑问式建议句型，收束于请求而非口号。）

1. `Dear President, \ I am a sophomore \ in the School of Foreign Languages.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 尊敬的校长，② 我是外国语学院的 ③ 一名大二学生。
   - **【重点词汇】**：sophomore `/ˈsɒfəmɔː/` [n.] 大二学生。

2. `Learning that the university plans to end all printing \ and drop printed textbooks, \ I welcome the direction \ but have two reservations, \ each with a suggested adjustment.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 得知学校计划全面终止打印业务并取消纸质教材后，② 我赞同这一大方向，③ 但保留两点顾虑，④ 并针对每点各提出一条改进建议。
   - **【加分句型】**：`Learning that...` 现在分词短语作状语；`each with a suggested adjustment` 独立主格结构。
   - **【重点词汇】**：reservation `/ˌrezəˈveɪʃn/` [n.] 保留意见，顾虑；adjustment `/əˈdʒʌstmənt/` [n.] 调整，改进。

3. `My first concern \ is fairness.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 我的第一点顾虑 ② 是教育公平。
   - **【重点词汇】**：fairness `/ˈfeənəs/` [n.] 公平，公正。

4. `However carefully the plan is designed, \ students without a reliable device or a stable connection \ would fall behind \ from the first week of classes.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 无论方案设计得多么周全，② 那些没有可靠设备或稳定网络连接的学生，③ 从第一周起 ④ 就会掉队落后。
   - **【加分句型】**：`However carefully...` 让步从句（= No matter how carefully）；`would fall behind` 虚拟推论。
   - **【重点词汇】**：reliable `/rɪˈlaɪəbl/` [adj.] 可靠的；fall behind 掉队。

5. `I therefore recommend that the university \ keep printed copies of every core textbook available on loan \ and lend tablets \ to students in need.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 因此我建议学校 ② 保留每本核心教材的纸质复本供借阅，③ 并向有困难的学生 ④ 出借平板电脑。
   - **【加分句型】**：`recommend that + (should) 动词原形` 虚拟语气。
   - **【重点词汇】**：on loan 供借阅；in need 有困难的。

6. `My second concern \ is deep reading.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 我的第二点顾虑 ② 是深度阅读。

7. `Unless students can still write in the margins \ and flip back and forth freely, \ their grasp of long, demanding texts \ is bound to weaken.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 除非学生依然能在页边随手批注 ② 并自由前后翻看，③ 否则他们对篇幅较长、难度较高的文本的掌握 ④ 必然会削弱。
   - **【加分句型】**：`Unless... be bound to...` 条件与必然因果。
   - **【重点词汇】**：margin `/ˈmɑːdʒɪn/` [n.] 页边空白；demanding `/dɪˈmɑːndɪŋ/` [adj.] 费力高要求的；be bound to 必然会。

8. `A workable adjustment \ would be a hybrid rule: \ printed readers for core courses \ and digital files for electives.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 一个切实可行的调整方案 ② 将是推行“混合规则”：③ 核心课程采用纸质读本，④ 选修课程采用电子文件。
   - **【重点词汇】**：workable `/ˈwɜːkəbl/` [adj.] 切实可行的；hybrid `/ˈhaɪbrɪd/` [adj.] 混合的；elective `/ɪˈlektɪv/` [n.] 选修课。

9. `Would it be possible \ to pilot the plan in two schools \ before it is applied campus-wide? \ I would be glad to join such a trial.`
   - **【标号翻译顺序】**：① 是否有可能 ② 在全校推广之前 ③ 先在两个学院进行试点？④ 我将非常乐意参与这样的试验。
   - **【重点词汇】**：pilot `/ˈpaɪlət/` [v.] 试验，试点；trial `/ˈtraɪəl/` [n.] 试验。

<summary>Passage One 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

For thirty years the distance an average American drives each day has barely changed, while the share of trips taken on foot has fallen to about one in fifty. Researchers in Melbourne decided to test whether the built environment could push that number the other way, and their findings are now changing how several cities approve new housing. The study followed more than two thousand adults for three years, mapping every street within a kilometer of their homes and asking them to record where they actually went on foot. <summary>Passage Two 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

Ask a student how to prepare for an examination and most will answer with late nights and strong coffee. Ask a neuroscientist and you are likely to hear the opposite. During deep sleep the brain runs through the day's events at high speed, a process researchers call consolidation, and in doing so it carries fragile new information out of a temporary holding area and into permanent storage. In one well-known experiment, volunteers learned a sequence of finger taps and were tested twelve hours later. Half of them slept during that interval and half stayed awake. The rested group improved by roughly ten percent on average, and the gains were still there a week later, while repetition with no sleep in between produced almost nothing. A second finding is stranger, because rest appears to choose. Not everything learned between breakfast and bedtime survives; cues presented around a study session, such as a particular smell or a brief tone, can tip which traces are kept, and anything tagged as emotionally important is pushed to the front of the queue. That helps explain why a student who works four hours and then sleeps often does better than one who works eight and does not. It also explains a cruelty in the same mechanism: an all-nighter damages recall of precisely the material the extra hours were meant to protect.

**27. B) credit**　语法依据：`earns a ___ that can be saved and spent later`，冠词 a 后需可数名词单数。判定证据：save and spend 是货币/记账额度（credit）的专属搭配链——credit 先"存"后"花"，正对应时间银行"存时间、花时间"机制。干扰 M) reward（一次性报酬，只能 receive/collect，不能被 save up 并当作额度 spend 出去，不可循环）；O) transaction（"saved and spent"语义不可能）；A) belonging（不可数，a 后不立）。

**28. D) emerged**　语法依据：`The idea ___ in Japan in the 1970s`，in the 1970s 要求一般过去时主动词，其后无宾语（不及物），主语为单一抽象概念"起源"义。干扰 F) merged（"融合"需两个以上对象，主语 the idea 单一）；E) linked（需宾语或 to/with 补足语）；H) notice（主语须有生命，且"想法注意到"不通）。
