### 参考译文

In the past, courier trucks came only as far as the township, and villagers often had to ride an electric scooter for half an hour to collect a package. Over the past two years, China has been working to improve its three-tier logistics network at the county, township and village levels and to bring express delivery to the villages, so that parcels are now delivered straight to the village entrance. Many areas have pooled passenger buses, postal vehicles and supply and marketing cooperative trucks to carry parcels along the way, solving the problems of high per-parcel delivery costs and scattered routes. In some villages in Shaanxi Province, a pickup point has been set up both in the corner shop and in the yard of the village committee; villagers simply quote a code to get their parcels, and mountain produce sent out the same morning can leave the village that evening. With the routes now open, apples, walnuts and honey from the mountains sell into online shopping carts across the country, while home appliances and seeds from the cities move smoothly into the villages. As of the end of the first half of this year, more than ninety percent of administrative villages nationwide had access to express delivery, and the rural parcel volume has grown by double digits for several years running.

### 得分点

1. **"过去，快递车只到乡镇" 的过去时态 + 范围终点**（In the past, courier trucks came only as far as the township, and villagers often had to ride an electric scooter for half an hour to collect a package）：as far as 表"只到……为止"是本卷首句考点，写 only arrived in the town 只给基础分；"常常要"often had to do；"骑半小时电动车"ride an electric scooter **for half an hour**（for + 时间段）；"去镇上（取件）"以不定式 to collect a package 表目的，勿写成 for collecting。乡镇 township 与村 village 两级不可混用。
2. **"近两年 + 着手完善"必用完成时**（Over the past two years, China has been working to improve…）：over/in the past two years 与现在完成（进行）时锁定搭配，全篇误用一般过去时累计扣分；"着手做某事"work to do / start working on；"三级物流网络"three-tier logistics network，"县、乡、村"以介词短语后置 at the county, township and village levels 表达，勿写成 county-town-village network。
3. **政策专名"快递进村"的意译 + so that 结果句**（bring express delivery to the villages, so that parcels are now delivered straight to the village entrance）：音译 kuaidi village-in 不得分，须意译；"让……直接送到"用被动 are delivered **straight to**（straight 表"直接地"）；"村口"the village entrance / the village gate 均可；推进另译 promote / step up the program of… 亦可给分。
4. **"把客运班车、邮政车和供销社货车拼起来捎货" 高分动词 + 现在分词表结果**（Many areas have pooled… trucks **to carry parcels along the way**, **solving the problems of…**）：pool 表"统筹拼合共用"是本卷拉开档次的选词，put together / share 给基础分；"捎货"carry parcels along the way 地道；solving… 现在分词作"自然而然的结果"状语，优于另起 and this solved；注意 solve 的宾语用 the problem(s) of + 名词短语。
5. **"单件配送成本高、路线分散" 两个名词短语并列**（high per-parcel delivery costs and scattered routes）："单件配送成本"per-parcel delivery cost / the cost of delivering a single parcel；"路线分散"scattered routes（-ed 形容词 scattered 为大纲词，写 routes are many and far 属汉式扣分）；并列时 cost 用复数、routes 用复数，保持结构对称。
6. **"都设了代收点" 无主语句转被动 + 分号并列两个小句**（a pickup point **has been set up** both in the corner shop and in the yard of the village committee; villagers simply **quote a code to** get their parcels…）：中文"设了"未点明施动者，英文须补 be set up（现在完成被动，与上文时态呼应），both…and 并列两处地点；"报个取件码即可拿到"用 quote a code to do sth（quote 表"报出、出示"为高分动词，say / read a code 给基础分）；后半句 mountain produce **sent out the same morning** can leave the village that evening 中过去分词短语作后置定语，是本卷长句给分点。专有名词按卷面提示写 pickup point、mountain produce；"村委会"the village committee；"陕西"Shaanxi（区别于"山西"Shanxi），"供销社"supply and marketing cooperative。
7. **"路一通……也……" with 复合结构 + while 对比并列**（**With the routes now open**, apples, walnuts and honey from the mountains **sell into** online shopping carts across the country, **while** home appliances and seeds from the cities move smoothly into the villages.）："路一通"用 With + 名词 + 形容词/分词复合结构最简，Once the routes are open 亦可；"卖进了各地的购物车"以 sell into online shopping carts across the country 转达电商意象，写 sold to the whole country 属信息丢失；while 表对比并列（非"当……时"），from the mountains / from the cities 两组后置定语互相呼应。
8. **"截至今年上半年" 时态分层 + 数据表达**（**As of** the end of the first half of this year, more than ninety percent of administrative villages nationwide **had access to** express delivery, and the rural parcel volume **has grown by double digits for several years running**.）："九成以上"more than / over ninety percent of（90% 亦可）；"通达快递"用 have access to express delivery，access 与 to 的搭配为高频得分点，写成 have express delivery 只给部分分；"行政村"administrative villages；时态分层——"截至某过去时点"用一般过去时 had access，"连续多年"延续至今用现在完成时 has grown **by** double digits（grow by + 增长幅度）for several years running；全篇美式拼写统一（program、-ize 与 -or 类拼法），无 colour / practise / organise 类英式形。
