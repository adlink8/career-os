Dear Professor Hu,

I am writing on behalf of the Student Union to invite you to open this year's Safety Education Week.

Many campus accidents happen in dormitories, where nobody present knows what to do in the first five minutes. Your course on emergency care is popular on campus, so **we can think of no one better to turn those minutes into saved lives.**

**The talk is planned for the evening of October 18 in the Student Center Hall, which seats 500 and has both a projector and a training dummy.** We would keep the lecture to 45 minutes, **leaving 25 minutes for questions and for the chest compressions you would demonstrate.** **Should this date inconvenience you, we would gladly move the whole week to fit your schedule.**

**May I also ask you to send us the title and a short biography by October 4?** **It would mean a great deal to us if you could accept.** I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

<summary>Passage One 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

In 1795 the French government offered a prize of twelve thousand francs to anyone who could supply armies with food that would not spoil on the march. A Parisian confectioner named Nicolas Appert took up the problem and spent fourteen years failing at it. His method, announced in 1810, was simple and workable: food sealed in glass bottles with cork and wax, then lowered into boiling water for a measured time. What he did not know, and what nobody could know for another fifty years, was why it worked, namely that heat was killing invisible life inside the jar and that the seal kept the rest out. Louis Pasteur's later experiments explained the part Appert had guessed at. Glass was awkward for soldiers, so two London publishers took out a patent for containers of iron plated with tin, and the metal can was born. Remarkably, the opener for it had to wait half a century. Instructions printed on early tins told the user to cut a hole with a bayonet or to drive in a chisel and a hammer, and collectors still pay heavily for cans that were never touched. By the American Civil War, factories were sealing tens of millions of tins a year, and for the first time a city family could eat summer peas in February.

<summary>Passage Three 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

A tide clock on a harbor wall looks broken, because its single hand points to high water hours away from what the ordinary clock says. It is not broken. The ocean answers the moon, which rises about fifty minutes later each night, so a shoreline keeps a schedule that drifts against ours. Creatures living between the waves follow that same schedule. When biologists carried crabs from an estuary into a laboratory tank, far from any tide, and kept the room dark and quiet, the animals still scrambled toward the surface at the hours when their home beach would have been bare. Their calendar was built inside them. Human beings keep two clocks, not one. The master pacemaker sits deep in the brain, is reset by morning light, and holds us to a day slightly longer than twenty-four hours, which we correct at every dawn. But the liver, the heart and the pancreas run on timetables of their own, set less by light than by the hour we eat. That is why a night shift harms people more than lost sleep suggests: the brain has been moved onto a new clock while the rest of the body reads an old one. Researchers who looked for a lunar influence on human sleep found effects far weaker than in crabs, and mostly in communities without electric light. The tide clocks keep turning on the harbor wall, and the fishers who read them are the only people there obeying two calendars at once.

### 参考译文

Mooncakes are an indispensable part of the Mid-Autumn Festival, and their round shape stands for the reunion of the whole family. In the past, every family would make mooncakes by hand for the festival; today, the vast majority of them come off automated production lines, and one large plant can bake several hundred thousand a day in the busy season. To attract young consumers, manufacturers have kept traditional fillings such as lotus seed paste and red bean paste while bringing out new flavors, and they have also created versions that are lower in sugar and packed in smaller pieces. A mature industry chain has grown around seasonal foods such as the mooncake, covering packaging, delivery and online sales. As the rules against excessive packaging are enforced, mooncakes that are affordable and plainly wrapped have become the mainstay of the market again. For many families, no matter how the flavors change, sharing a mooncake with their loved ones on Mid-Autumn night remains the most solemn ritual.

### 得分点

1. **"象征着一家人团圆" 与并列句**（and their round shape stands for the reunion of the whole family）：文化象征句用一般现在时；stand for / symbolize 均可给分，"团圆"写 the reunion of the whole family 或 family reunion，直译 roundly get together 不得分。
2. **"过去……都会……" 的过去习惯**（In the past, every family would make mooncakes by hand）：would + 动词原形表过去反复出现的动作，是 used to 的高分替换；分号并置 In the past…; today,… 构成今昔对比，比硬凑 and 更紧凑。
3. **"出自工厂的自动化生产线" + 数量表达**（the vast majority of them come off automated production lines, and one large plant can bake several hundred thousand a day in the busy season）：come off 表示产品"从（生产线）下来"属地道动词选词；"绝大多数"the vast majority of；"数十万"several hundred thousand，写成 tens of thousands（数万）算数字失分；"节前"处理为 in the busy season 属得体意译。
4. **"为吸引……" 不定式目的状语 + "在保留……的同时"**（To attract young consumers … have kept traditional fillings such as lotus seed paste and red bean paste while bringing out new flavors）：句首不定式表目的；while + 现在分词的省略结构是四级高频句式，比另起 While they keep… 更简洁；"馅料"按卷面提示写 filling，"莲蓉/豆沙"lotus seed paste / red bean paste。
5. **"做出低糖和小份的样式" 定语从句**（versions that are lower in sugar and packed in smaller pieces）：先行词 versions + 定语从句，内含 are lower in… 与（are）packed in 两个并列谓语；"低糖"必须写 low in sugar / lower in sugar，堆砌成 low sugar food 扣分。
6. **"围绕月饼等节令食品……形成一条成熟的产业链" 完成时 + 分词补充**（A mature industry chain has grown around seasonal foods such as the mooncake, covering packaging, delivery and online sales）：现在完成时表"已经形成"；such as 举出月饼作例子，"节令食品"seasonal food 是本主题核心文化词；covering 引出的分词短语展开三个环节，是本卷拉开层次的关键句；"电商销售"online sales / e-commerce，"产业链"按卷面提示写 industry chain。
7. **"随着……规定落实" 与"重新成为市场主流"**（As the rules against excessive packaging are enforced, mooncakes that are affordable and plainly wrapped have become the mainstay of the market again）：as 引导时间/伴随状语从句（With the rules … being enforced 亦可），从句用一般现在时被动表"正在被落实"；主句用现在完成时，其主语带 that 定语从句；"过度包装"按卷面提示写 excessive packaging，"价格亲民"affordable / reasonably priced，"包装简朴"plainly wrapped / simply packaged。
8. **"无论……仍……" 让步 + 动名词作主语**（no matter how the flavors change, sharing a mooncake with their loved ones on Mid-Autumn night remains the most solemn ritual）：no matter how / however + 从句；主句主语是动名词短语 sharing…，谓语须用第三人称单数 remains，这是本卷最易失分处。专有名词 the Mid-Autumn Festival、mooncake 须拼写正确；全篇美式拼写 flavor（非 flavour）。
