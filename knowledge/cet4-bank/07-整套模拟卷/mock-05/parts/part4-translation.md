“三伏天”是一年中最闷热的一段日子，多在七月中旬到八月中旬，前后约四十天。民间素有“夏病夏治”的说法，即趁着一年中阳气最盛时，把冬天易复发的老毛病提前调理一番。最有名的当数“三伏贴”：医生把中药研成细末，调成膏状，贴在后背和手臂的<u>穴位</u>（acupoint）上，每次敷两个多小时。不少中医院在三伏天开设<u>养生</u>（wellness）门诊，针灸、拔罐和药浴的预约要排到一个月后。医生建议，闷热天气里不宜剧烈运动，出汗过多后应适量喝温水，切莫贪凉猛灌冰饮。也有人提醒，三伏贴并非人人适用，皮肤敏感者、孕妇和正在发烧的人应听从医生的判断。

### 参考译文

The Sanfu days, the hottest and most humid stretch of the year, usually run from mid-July to mid-August and last about forty days. A long-standing folk saying holds that winter illnesses can be treated in summer, which means taking advantage of the season when the body's yang energy is at its peak to get a head start on old ailments that tend to return in winter. The best-known practice is the Sanfu patch: a doctor grinds herbal medicine into a fine powder, mixes it into a paste and applies it to acupoints on the patient's back and arms for a little over two hours at a time. Many traditional Chinese medicine hospitals open special wellness clinics during the Sanfu days, and appointments for acupuncture, cupping and medicinal baths are already booked a month ahead. Doctors advise against strenuous exercise in such muggy weather and suggest drinking a moderate amount of warm water after heavy sweating instead of gulping down iced drinks. Others note that the patch does not suit everyone, and that people with sensitive skin, pregnant women and anyone running a fever should follow a doctor's judgment.

### 得分点

1. **同位语解释专有概念"三伏天"**（The Sanfu days, the hottest and most humid stretch of the year, …）：本卷首句考点。三伏无对应英文词，音译 Sanfu days 加同位语解释是最稳的处理；"闷热"hot and humid / muggy，写成 very hot weather 只给基础分；stretch 表"一段时间"为地道选词。
2. **"多在……，前后约四十天" 谓语并列 + 时间表达**（usually run from mid-July to mid-August and last about forty days）：run from…to… 表日程"跨"某区间，与 and last… 共用一个主语，避免写成两句 It is usually in… It lasts…；"约四十天"about forty days，"前后"含在区间里，不必另译。
3. **"民间素有……的说法" + 宾语从句**（A long-standing folk saying holds that…）：hold that 是"（说法）认为"的高分动词，优于 There is a saying says（语法错）；"夏病夏治"意译 winter illnesses can be treated in summer，须用被动，直译 summer treat winter disease 不得分。
4. **"趁着……阳气最盛时，把……提前调理一番" 目的与定语叠加**（taking advantage of the season when the body's yang energy is at its peak to get a head start on old ailments that tend to return in winter）：本卷拉开档次的关键长句。which means + 动名词；when 定语从句修饰 the season；get a head start on"提前着手"对应"提前调理"，ailment 表"老毛病"比 disease 更贴切；"阳气"yang energy 可加解释或写 the body's yang energy，拼 heat of the body 只给部分分；句末 that tend to return in winter 修饰 ailments，注意谓语单数 tends。
5. **冒号展开 + 三个并列谓语**（The best-known practice is the Sanfu patch: a doctor grinds…, mixes… and applies…）："最有名的当数"译 The best-known practice / remedy；冒号后连动 grind…, mix… and apply… 时态一致、主语不重复，是"连续动作"标准句式；"研成细末"grind into a fine powder、"调成膏状"mix into a paste、"贴"apply to（非 stick on，后者口语）；"每次敷两个多小时"for a little over two hours at a time，"每次"at a time 不可漏，漏译算信息缺失。
6. **"开设……门诊" + "预约要排到一个月后" 并列句 + 被动**（Many traditional Chinese medicine hospitals open special wellness clinics during the Sanfu days, and appointments for… are already booked a month ahead）："中医院"traditional Chinese medicine hospitals（TCM hospitals 亦可）；"预约要排到一个月后"用被动 are booked a month ahead，写成 the reservations are very crowded 属汉式英语扣分；疗法三项并列 acupuncture（针灸）、cupping（拔罐）、medicinal baths（药浴）是本主题核心词汇，拼写错误影响得分。
7. **"不宜剧烈运动 / 切莫贪凉猛灌冰饮" 建议句式 + 对比**（Doctors advise against strenuous exercise in such muggy weather and suggest drinking a moderate amount of warm water after heavy sweating instead of gulping down iced drinks）：advise against + 名词、suggest + 动名词为高频搭配，两动词共用 Doctors 主语；"贪凉"以 instead of gulping down iced drinks 表"反其宜而行"，比 Don't drink cold water 更符合书面语域；"适量"a moderate/proper amount of；"剧烈运动"strenuous/vigorous exercise。
8. **"并非人人适用" 部分否定 + that 从句并列三类人群**（the patch does not suit everyone, and that people with sensitive skin, pregnant women and anyone running a fever should follow a doctor's judgment）：not…everyone 为部分否定，是本卷必考点，译成 suits nobody 属逻辑错；"皮肤敏感者"people with sensitive skin（用 with 而非 of）、"正在发烧的人"anyone running a fever；"听从医生的判断"follow a doctor's judgment / take a doctor's advice。全篇美式拼写统一（无 colour/practise/organise 类英式形），时态以一般现在时贯穿全段。
