### 满分范文（实测正文 160 词，不含标题；连标题 166 词，均在 120–180 区间内）

*Draw a line, not a ban*

Debates about AI-written assignments are usually framed as a question of honesty, but they are really a question of what a degree is for. Our position: AI may support our thinking; it may not replace it.

Grades measure practice, not just product. **If a machine supplies the argument, the student who submits it earns a mark but misses the exercise meant to change them.** Since teachers cannot reliably spot an AI draft, the line belongs in the rules.

**First, instructors should label every assignment as AI-free, AI-assisted or AI-required, so that no one has to guess.** **Second, a short oral check in class, where students explain how they reached their conclusions, would keep honest users safe and expose lazy ones.**

**Rules that name the tools we may use protect both the teachers who grade and the students who try.** **Used as a tutor rather than a ghostwriter, AI can sharpen a mind instead of rotting it.**

（亮点句型说明：① If 真实条件句 + earns…but misses… 对比式并列谓语；② label…as… 复合宾语 + so that 目的状语从句；③ 逗号隔开的 where 非限定定语从句作插入解释 + keep A safe / expose B 并列宾补；④ Rules that… 限定定语从句 + both…and… 双宾对称；⑤ 过去分词 Used as… 方式状语（与主语 AI 逻辑一致）+ rather than / instead of 双重对比。标题 Draw a line, not a ban 为社论常见的祈使式对举标题。全篇美式拼写与美式用词，无 -ise/programme 类英式形。）

<summary>Passage One 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

Lighthouses are among the oldest structures still doing the job they were built for, and their history is largely one of light getting brighter. The earliest famous beacon, at Alexandria around 280 BCE, burned wood and oil in an iron basket atop a tower, so it could guide a ship from seaward but could also be mistaken for a town burning on land. For centuries the improvement was organizational, not technical. Religious houses and trade guilds sold the right to display a light, took a fee from every vessel that passed, and hired a keeper to feed the flame and polish the glass. The real change came from optics. In 1789 a Swiss engineer built a lamp with a flat wick that burned steadier and whiter, and twenty years later an Irishman showed that a glass drum carved with parallel rings could bend a weak beam into a broad horizontal sheet visible far off, without extra fuel. It made a flame of a given size four times as useful, and every coast copied it within a generation. Oil gave way to gas and then to electric bulbs, and keepers disappeared: most towers today are watched from a shore station hundreds of kilometers away. What has not changed is the reason these buildings exist. A hidden rock, not the open sea, is the danger.

<summary>Passage Two 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

Farmers long said that bees told one another where the flowers were, and were laughed at. Karl von Frisch spent the 1940s proving them right. He painted marks on workers so that he could recognize individuals, trained them to sugar water at a table fifty meters away, and watched the comb. A successful forager ran forward in a narrow line, whipped her abdomen to one side, returned in a half circle, and repeated this a dozen times: the waggle dance. Two numbers come from it. The length of the straight run tracks how far the food lies, and about a second of running means roughly a kilometer. The angle of that line against gravity on the vertical comb gives direction away from the sun, so a dance sixty degrees right of straight up asks the listener to fly sixty degrees right of the sunlight. Bees on a comb in darkness, or in a hive tilted from vertical, make errors that match this rule precisely. A bee returning from a poor feeding site dances more slowly and with shorter runs, so the crowd around her cools off. Later work added that a returning bee carries the flower's scent on her body and that listeners taste what she brings, telling a recruit what to seek.

<summary>Passage Three 原文（Tapescript）</summary>

The moon raises and lowers the sea twice a day, and in a few places the gap between high and low water exceeds ten meters. That difference is energy, which engineers have tried to sell since the 1960s. The oldest method is a barrage: a wall across an estuary with gates that fill a basin at high tide and release it through turbines on the way out. La Rance in France has run this way since 1966, producing enough for several hundred thousand homes. It also costs a great deal to build, holds back sediment, and changes the mudflats wading birds need, which is why no scheme of that size has been approved in Europe since. Two newer approaches skip the wall. Tidal streams can turn underwater rotors, and since water is some eight hundred times denser than air, a machine the size of a car generates useful power from a flow far slower than a wind turbine needs. Such devices sit on the seabed and can stop during fish spawning seasons. A third design uses a floating column. The difficulty common to all is timing: output peaks twice a day and drifts across the clock, rarely lining up with the hours households boil water, so storage is needed.

### 参考译文

China is the home of tea, and tea growing and tea drinking here date back more than 4,000 years. For Chinese people, when guests come to their home, offering them a cup of hot tea first is the simplest yet most sincere form of hospitality. Making the tea, pouring it and presenting the cup with both hands may look easy, yet every step has its rules: water that is too hot will make the tea bitter, and the cup is filled only seven-tenths full, leaving the other three-tenths for friendship. As the host pours more water, he or she asks whether the journey has been tiring; the guest answers by tapping the table lightly with two fingers, a silent way of saying thanks. What matters in a cup of tea is not whether the tea is expensive, but that the host is willing to keep a whole afternoon free and to talk slowly with a friend who has come from far away. Today coffee and bottled drinks have entered the offices of young people, but inviting a guest to a cup of tea is still the safest way to open a family gathering or a business talk.

### 得分点

1. **"已有四千多年" + 表"回溯"的现在完成时**（date back more than 4,000 years / have a history of over 4,000 years）：历史句不得写成一般过去时（~~dated back~~ 意为"已终止"）；"故乡"译 the home of tea 最地道，hometown of tea 亦可给分；"种茶和饮茶"用 tea growing and tea drinking 并列动名词作主语。
2. **"对中国人来说……是……待客之道"主系表 + 最高级**（For Chinese people, … is the simplest yet most sincere form of hospitality）：全句主干是"奉茶是最朴素也最真诚的待客之道"，when guests come 为插入的情景从句；yet 连接两个最高级比 and 更能体现语气推进；"待客之道"按卷面提示写 hospitality，form / way of hosting guests 均可。
3. **"看似简单，每一步却有讲究"让步 + yet 转折**（… may look easy, yet every step has its rules）：三个动名词 Making the tea, pouring it and presenting the cup with both hands 并列作主语，"双手奉上"present … with both hands 是准确动作表达；"有讲究"译 have its rules / take particular care，直译 have 讲究 不得分。
4. **冒号解释句 + "水温太高，茶会发苦"**（: water that is too hot will make the tea bitter）：冒号后另起完整解释句是分段层次的关键；用定语从句 water that is too hot 或 if 条件句 If the water is too hot, the tea turns bitter 均可给分；"发苦"make … bitter / turn bitter 使役结构。
5. **"只斟七分，留下三分情谊"数字与分词结果状语**（the cup is filled only seven-tenths full, leaving the other three-tenths for friendship）：被动 be filled + 分数表达 seven-tenths，现在分词 leaving 引出"留下三分"的结果，是本段最能拉开档次的句式；"情谊"friendship / warmth，意译即可，不追求逐字对应。
6. **"一边……一边……"与"则"的对照结构**（As the host pours more water, he or she asks whether … ; the guest answers by tapping the table lightly with two fingers, a silent way of saying thanks）：as 引导时间状语对译"一边"，he or she 呼应"主人"；by doing 表方式，"轻叩"tap … lightly，词序错误（lightly tap the table 亦可，但 tap the table heavy 之类搭配错误扣分）；末尾 a silent way of saying thanks 为同位语，转达"无声地道谢"。
7. **"不在乎贵贱，要紧的是……"主语从句 + not…but… 对比**（What matters in a cup of tea is not whether the tea is expensive, but that the host is willing to keep a whole afternoon free and to talk slowly with a friend who has come from far away）：what 引导的主语从句 + not…but… 并列表语，but 后接 that 从句、be willing to 并列两个不定式；"空出一个下午"keep a whole afternoon free / set aside a whole afternoon；"远道而来的朋友"用限定定语从句 who has come from far away。
