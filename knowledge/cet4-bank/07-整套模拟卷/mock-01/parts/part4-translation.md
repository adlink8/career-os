### 参考译文

In recent years, with the acceleration of urban development, many cities in China have made great efforts to build greenways, providing citizens with pleasant places for relaxation and exercise. These greenways usually run along rivers, lakes or mountain ridges, connecting parks, squares and historic neighborhoods like strings of green necklaces. At weekends, the greenways are always crowded with people, with many running, cycling or taking a walk. Walking along a greenway, people can not only get some exercise but also enjoy the scenery and feel the changes in the city. The building of greenways has not only improved the ecological environment and raised the quality of life of local residents, but also promoted the development of tourism and sports industries in nearby areas. Experts believe that more and more greenways will become an important symbol of green development in Chinese cities.

### 得分点

1. **"近年来" + 现在完成时**（In recent years … have made great efforts）：本段主体时态为现在完成时，全篇误用一般过去时累计扣分；"大力修建"译作 make great efforts to build / build … on a large scale 均可给分。
2. **"为市民提供了……"分词结构**（…, providing citizens with…）：现在分词作结果/伴随状语是四级核心句型，比另起一句 and it provides 更能体现句式掌控力；提供搭配 provide sb with sth。
3. **"沿……延伸 / 把……连接起来"**（run along… / connecting parks, squares and historic neighborhoods）：run along 表示线路"沿……延伸"属地道动词选词；connect…与前面延伸句以分词并置，避免连词堆砌。
4. **比喻处理"就像一条条绿色的项链"**（like strings of green necklaces）：介词 like 引导明喻，结构完整即可给分，不追求逐字对应。
5. **"人头攒动"的意译**（be crowded with people）：考查习语转达能力，直译人头晃动不得分；with many running, cycling or taking a walk 的 with 复合结构为加分句式。
6. **"既可以……也可以……"**（can not only get some exercise but also enjoy…）：not only…but also 并列两个动词短语，平行结构对称即给分；"沿途的风景"译 the scenery along the way / the scenery。
7. **三重并列"不仅……，……，还……"**（has not only improved… and raised…, but also promoted…）：长句分层能力，not only 后并置两个完成时动词、but also 引出第三层，是本卷拉开档次的关键句。
8. **热点_topic_词汇**：生态环境 ecological environment、生活质量 quality of life、绿色发展 green development、重要标志 an important symbol；"专家认为"用 Experts believe that + 宾语从句。专有名词"绿道"按卷面提示写 greenway，无需自造译名。
