## 📖 Reading Passage: Wearable Health Trackers: The Quantification of Everyday Wellness (可穿戴健康追踪器：日常健康的“量化自我”迷思)

In an era marked by rapid health awareness and ubiquitous technology, wearable fitness trackers have evolved from niche athletic gadgets into mainstream lifestyle accessories. Millions of consumers diligently strap smartwatches to their wrists each morning, monitoring daily step counts, resting heart rates, and blood oxygen saturation with quasi-religious devotion. The philosophy propelling this boom is known as the 'quantified self'—the belief that by translating continuous bodily functions into objective digital metrics, individuals can eliminate ambiguity, optimize vitality, and assume full accountability for their longevity.

Undeniably, automated biological tracking offers tangible public health advantages. For individuals managing chronic conditions such as hypertension or cardiovascular arrhythmias, continuous biometric tracking provides critical early warning indicators, prompting timely medical intervention. Furthermore, the gamified mechanics of digital trackers—such as virtual trophies and milestone streaks—serve as potent psychological catalysts that incentivize previously sedentary office workers to incorporate brisk walking and cardiovascular exercise into their daily routines.

Yet, clinical psychologists have begun warning against the unintended emotional fallout of this excessive quantification. When individuals rely exclusively on wrist-worn monitors to interpret their physiological status, they frequently lose touch with intuitive bodily sensations. Sleep specialists have recently coined the diagnostic term 'orthosomnia' to describe an emerging anxiety disorder characterized by obsessive preoccupation with achieving flawless sleep tracker scores. Patients suffering from this condition report lying awake in bed, immobilized by anxiety that their restless movement will ruin their nightly recovery algorithm—ironically precipitating the very insomnia they sought to prevent.

Moreover, commercial algorithms often oversimplify the fluid, highly individualized nature of human physiology. Rigid benchmarks—such as the arbitrary ten-thousand-step threshold popularized by early marketing campaigns—fail to account for biological diversity, daily stress variations, and organic recovery needs. True wellness is not a static mathematical equation to be solved through obsessive algorithmic surveillance. Instead of allowing automated scores to dictate daily self-esteem, we must learn to integrate digital insights with mindful body awareness, recognizing that a vibrant life encompasses natural fluctuations that no sensor can perfectly capture.

### 逐题深度深度解析与陷阱剖析

**第 1 题 (正确答案: B)**
- **题干定位**：1. What is the fundamental premise behind the "quantified self" movement described in Paragraph 1?
- **命题解密**：第一段尾句明确指出：`the quantified self—the belief that by translating continuous bodily functions into objective digital metrics, individuals can eliminate ambiguity, optimize vitality, and assume full accountability for their longevity.`
- **选项对齐**：选项 B 中的 `Translating bodily functions into digital metrics enables individuals to optimize health` 与原文在句式与核心实词上高度对应（`translating continuous bodily functions into objective digital metrics` 对应前半句，`optimize vitality` 对应 `optimize health`）。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项 `depends entirely on expensive digital equipment` 犯了“绝对化”（entirely）与无中生有错误；
  - C 项 `cure chronic cardiovascular diseases automatically` 夸大功能，手表仅能监测无法自动治愈疾病；
  - D 项 `replace traditional diagnosis with smartphone apps` 原文未提及取代医生。

**第 2 题 (正确答案: A)**
- **题干定位**：2. The word "sedentary" in Paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to _______.
- **命题解密**：第二段上下文：`incentivize previously sedentary office workers to incorporate brisk walking and cardiovascular exercise into their daily routines`。
- **选项对齐**：考查动宾与对比反衬逻辑。追踪器的游戏化机制激励这些员工去进行 `brisk walking and cardiovascular exercise`（快步走和心血管有氧锻炼），说明他们“原本缺乏身体活动、长期久坐”，故 `sedentary` 等价于 A 项 `physically inactive`。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - B 项 `highly ambitious`（雄心勃勃的）、C 项 `intellectually curious`（求知欲强的）、D 项 `emotionally unstable`（情绪不稳定的）均与上下文的身体运动情境无关，属于无关品质干扰。

**第 3 题 (正确答案: C)**
- **题干定位**：3. How does "orthosomnia" paradoxically worsen sleep quality according to Paragraph 3?
- **命题解密**：第三段后半部分对“正睡症”患者的行为机制做出详细描述：`Patients suffering from this condition report lying awake in bed, immobilized by anxiety that their restless movement will ruin their nightly recovery algorithm—ironically precipitating the very insomnia they sought to prevent.`
- **选项对齐**：患者因极度害怕自己的翻身活动会拉低手表评分，导致焦虑得整夜不敢动弹，反而直接诱发了失眠。这与 C 项 `Anxiety about achieving perfect sleep metrics keeps patients awake` 构成精准同义替换。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项（屏幕太亮照亮卧室）、B 项（深睡眠时设备发出刺耳警报）、D 项（发射电磁波干扰褪黑素分泌）皆属于脱离文章主旨的常识臆造干扰。

**第 4 题 (正确答案: B)**
- **题干定位**：4. What flaw in commercial fitness algorithms is highlighted in Paragraph 4?
- **命题解密**：第四段首句先总括指出算法过度简化了人体的动态与个体化特征（`oversimplify the fluid, highly individualized nature of human physiology`），第二句紧接着指斥具体硬指标：`Rigid benchmarks—such as the arbitrary ten-thousand-step threshold popularized by early marketing campaigns—fail to account for biological diversity, daily stress variations, and organic recovery needs.`
- **选项对齐**：原文 `Rigid benchmarks... arbitrary... fail to account for biological diversity` 与 B 项 `They impose rigid, arbitrary thresholds that ignore individual biological diversity` 词义一一重合。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项（过快消耗手表电量）、C 项（容易受到恶意黑客攻击）、D 项（鼓励用户喝过多含糖运动饮料）皆为凭空编造，无文本依据。

**第 5 题 (正确答案: B)**
- **题干定位**：5. What does the author suggest regarding personal wellness in the conclusion?
- **命题解密**：末段最后一句作者亮明自身立场与解决方案：`Instead of allowing automated scores to dictate daily self-esteem, we must learn to integrate digital insights with mindful body awareness, recognizing that a vibrant life encompasses natural fluctuations that no sensor can perfectly capture.`
- **选项对齐**：作者明确提倡将“数字洞察”与“清晰敏锐的身体内在感知”相结合，对应 B 选项 `Individuals should combine digital metrics with intuitive body awareness`（`integrate` 替换为 `combine`，`digital insights` 替换为 `digital metrics`，`mindful body awareness` 替换为 `intuitive body awareness`）。
- **陷阱辨析**：
  - A 项 `discard all health trackers immediately` 走向极端，文章第二段已明确肯定过其公共健康红利；
  - C 项（医保强制佩戴）与 D 项（划归为处方医疗器械）在原文中均未提及。
