## 📖 Reading Passage: The Devaluation of Sleep: Cultural Shifts in Modern Rest Attitudes (睡眠贬值：现代社会对休息态度的文化变迁与生理反噬)

For millions of years of mammalian evolution, sleep was honored as an unassailable biological rhythm governing daily existence. In pre-industrial human communities, sleep was fluid and respected, with historical records detailing 'biphasic sleep' patterns where individuals awoke for an hour of quiet nocturnal reflection before resuming rest. However, with the advent of the Industrial Revolution and the widespread electrification of cities, sleep was aggressively re-conceptualized. In a society obsessed with relentless mechanical productivity, natural rest began to be moralized as a wasteful biological defect—a shameful concession to bodily weakness.

This cultural hostility toward sleep reached its ideological zenith in the late twentieth century, epitomized by corporate slogans declaring that 'greed never sleeps' and modern professionals bragging about surviving on four hours of rest. Sacrificing sleep became the preeminent badge of ruthless personal ambition. Sleep was treated as an elastic, expendable resource that could be indefinitely truncated to accommodate midnight work emails, early gym sessions, and nocturnal leisure. Those who prioritized eight hours of sleep were often dismissed as lacking professional dedication or entrepreneurial vigor.

Yet, contemporary neurobiology has decisively debunked this industrial folklore, revealing that chronic sleep deprivation inflicts devastating physiological destruction. A pioneering discovery by neuroscientists at the University of Rochester demonstrated that during deep non-REM sleep, the brain's 'glymphatic system' activates, acting as a biological waste-clearance mechanism that flushes out toxic metabolic byproducts, including amyloid proteins associated with Alzheimer's disease. Depriving the brain of sufficient restorative sleep is not an act of heroic discipline; rather, it poisons neural architecture, impairs emotional regulation, and drastically escalates long-term cardiovascular mortality.

Reversing this sleep deficit requires a profound cultural paradigm shift. Corporations must dismantle the perverse incentives that reward nocturnal burnout, replacing round-the-clock connectivity expectations with mandatory digital quiet hours. Public health authorities must educate citizens that sleep is not an unproductive void, but the indispensable foundation of creative cognition, cellular repair, and emotional resilience. True human vitality requires recognizing that rest is not the reward for completed labor, but the vital biological prerequisite that makes meaningful labor possible.

### 🏮 全文逐段地道精翻

在数百万年的哺乳动物漫长进化史中，睡眠一直被尊为统治生命日常运转的不可撼动的生物节律。在前工业时代的人类社群中，睡眠节奏顺应自然且备受尊崇；历史典籍详细记载了“双相睡眠”的古老模式：人们在深夜醒来享受一个小时静谧沉思的安宁，随后再重回梦乡。然而，伴随着工业革命的降临与城市电网照明的普照，睡眠的概念遭到了暴力的彻底重构。在一个对机械化生产效率产生病态迷恋的社会里，自然的休整开始被拔高到道德层面被斥为一种浪费性的生理缺陷——是对肉体软弱的耻辱妥协。

这种对睡眠的文化敌视在20世纪末达到了意识形态的巅峰，其缩影便是华尔街“贪婪永不眠”的口号，以及职场精英以每天仅睡4小时为荣的病态炫耀。剥夺压缩睡眠成了彰显个人残酷进取野心的至高徽章。睡眠被降格为一种富有弹性、随时可弃的廉价消耗品，可以被随意截断挤压以腾出时间回复半夜邮件、赶清晨健身打卡或进行报复性熬夜。那些坚持保证8小时睡眠的人，往往被污名化为缺乏职业进取心或创业激情的平庸之辈。

然而，当代神经生物学彻底粉碎了这种工业社会的荒谬民间迷信，揭示出长期的睡眠剥夺会给身体招致毁灭性的生理瓦解。罗切斯特大学神经科学家的一项开创性研究证实：在深度非快动眼睡眠阶段，大脑内部的“类淋巴系统”会被激活，它化身一套精密的生理排毒清洁机制，高效冲洗掉包括与阿尔茨海默病直接相关的淀粉样蛋白在内的毒性代谢废物。剥夺大脑必须的深度修复睡眠绝非英勇自律的美德，而是在无情投毒神经脑组织、摧毁情绪控制中枢，并成倍拉高长期心血管致死率。

扭转这场全民性的“睡眠赤字”，需要一场深刻的社会文化范式重塑。企业机构必须废除那些奖励熬夜透支的畸形考勤机制，用强制性的数字离线静音时段替代随时随地连线的全天候待命预期。公共卫生部门必须向公众普及：睡眠绝非生产力真空的空白浪费，而是激发创造性思维、细胞再生修复以及情绪坚韧度的命脉基石。真正生机盎然的人类生命活力要求我们深刻领悟：休息绝非完成劳作后的恩赐奖赏，而是让一切有意义的创造性劳作成为可能的根本生理前提。
