## 📖 Reading Passage: Microplastics in Marine Ecosystems: An Invisible Global Crisis (海洋微塑料与食物链传递：隐匿的全球生态危机)

In the public imagination, marine plastic pollution is predominantly visualized as vast offshore floating debris islands, such as the infamous Great Pacific Garbage Patch, where discarded plastic bottles, fishing nets, and packaging containers mar the pristine ocean surface. While macroscopic trash undeniably poses acute hazards to marine megafauna, marine biologists have discovered that the far more insidious environmental threat is microscopic. Millions of metric tons of plastic waste are constantly fragmenting under solar ultraviolet radiation, oceanic wave action, and thermal degradation into ubiquitous particles smaller than five millimeters—known to science as 'microplastics.'

The fundamental ecological peril of microplastics lies in their seamless infiltration of marine food webs. Due to their diminutive size, microscopic synthetic fragments are indiscriminately consumed by primary oceanic grazers, including zooplankton, krill, and larval fish, who mistake brightly colored particles for microscopic algae. Once ingested, these indigestible synthetic particles cause physical intestinal blockages, reduce natural appetite, and stunt growth. More catastrophically, microplastics act as chemical sponges, absorbing concentrated persistent toxic pollutants—such as DDT and polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs)—from surrounding seawater, before leaching these hazardous synthetic chemicals into animal tissue.

Furthermore, through an ecological mechanism known as 'biomagnification,' toxins absorbed by organisms at the bottom of the food web multiply in concentration as they travel up the trophic ladder. Small fish are devoured by larger predators, which are subsequently harvested for global seafood markets. Recent clinical toxicology analyses conducted across leading research hospitals have confirmed the presence of microplastic particles and associated chemical plasticizers in human bloodstream samples, lung tissue, and even maternal placentas, raising grave medical concerns regarding chronic cellular inflammation, endocrine disruption, and immune dysfunction.

Tackling this invisible oceanic emergency requires confronting the root source of synthetic plastic proliferation. Fragmented beach cleanups and coastal trash barriers, while commendable, represent mere downstream bandages on an open wound. The international community must establish legally binding global plastic treaties that impose enforceable caps on virgin polymer production, mandate biodegradable packaging alternatives, and enforce severe financial liability on fossil fuel conglomerates. Humanity must recognize that the health of the global oceans is intimately and indivisibly linked to human biological survival.

### 🏮 全文逐段地道精翻

在大众的普遍想象中，海洋塑料垃圾污染往往被直观地具象化为大洋深处漂浮的庞大垃圾岛——例如臭名昭著的“太平洋垃圾带”，废弃的塑料水瓶、破烂的渔网和包装盒粗暴地玷污着原本纯净蔚蓝的洋面。尽管肉眼可见的大型塑料垃圾无疑对海洋大型脊椎动物构成了致命威胁，但海洋生物学家发现：真正更具潜伏性与毁灭性的环境毒瘤其实隐藏在微观世界中。数以百万吨计的废弃塑料在强烈太阳紫外线暴晒、海浪剧烈机械撞击以及热降解的作用下，不断粉碎成数以万亿计小于5毫米的微小颗粒——科学界将其统称为“微塑料”。

微塑料最根本的生态浩劫，在于它们能毫无阻碍地无缝渗入海洋整个食物链网络。由于微塑料体积极为微小，浮游动物、磷虾以及幼鱼等海洋初级滤食生物往往不分青红皂白地将其吞食，常常误将色彩斑斓的微塑料颗粒当成微藻美食。一旦被摄入腹中，这些根本无法消化的石化颗粒不仅会引发严重的肠道物理梗阻、抑制正常的进食欲望并导致生长发育停滞。更为致命的是，微塑料宛如一块块吸毒的“化学海绵”，能够疯狂富集并吸附周边海水中高浓度的持久性有机毒素（如DDT和多氯联苯PCBs），随后将这些致命的合成化学毒素源源不断地析出并渗透进海洋生物机体组织。

不仅如此，通过生态学上被称为“生物放大作用（biomagnification）”的残酷机制，位于食物链底端生物所吸收的毒素，在沿着营养级逐级向上传递的过程中其浓度会呈几何级数成倍暴增。微小鱼虾被中型捕食者吞食，而中型鱼类又沦为大型鱼类的盘中餐，最终被捕捞送上全球人类的海鲜餐桌。全球顶尖科研医院近期开展的多项临床毒理学检测，已经确凿无疑地在人类的血液样本、肺部活检组织甚至新生儿母体胎盘中，检测出了微塑料颗粒与相关塑化剂残留，这引发了医学界关于慢性细胞炎症、内分泌紊乱以及免疫系统功能崩溃的空前担忧。

遏制这场肉眼不可见的深海生态海啸，必须敢于向石化塑料的源头滥用动真格。零敲碎打的周末海滩拾垃圾活动或沿海防污拦污栅栏，固然值得肯定，但终究只是在溃烂伤口上贴的一张治标不治本的创可贴。国际社会必须通力合作，缔结具有强制法律效力的全球塑料治理公约，从源头上对原生塑料聚合物的产能设定硬性天花板，强制推广可生物降解的环保替代包装，并向化石燃料跨国企业施加严厉的生态环境索赔责任。全人类必须清醒地意识到：浩瀚大洋的生态健康，与人类自身的生物学繁衍存亡骨肉相连、休戚与共。
